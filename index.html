The problem

Most local websites lose customers before they call.

01

They load slowly on phones.

Heavy templates and oversized images make people wait. Many visitors leave before the page finishes loading.

02

Contacting you takes too long.

If a visitor can't find your number or a quote button quickly, they go back to search and call a competitor.

We build around both problems from the first sketch: lean pages, plain wording, and a call button within reach on every screen.
